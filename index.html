Phishing statistics

Daily Emails

Approximately 3.4 billion phishing emails circulate daily (around 39,000 per second).

Attack frequency

Breach costs

AI integration

Over 80% of recent phishing emails leverage generative AI tools to craft hyper-personalized messages, driving higher click-through rates.

Time to Contain

It takes an average of 254 days to identify and contain a breach that originates from a phishing vector.

Compromised accounts
